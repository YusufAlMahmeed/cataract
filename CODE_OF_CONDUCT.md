# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for
everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity
and expression, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race,
religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:

- Being respectful of differing viewpoints and experiences.
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback.
- Focusing on what is best for the community.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, insulting or derogatory comments,
publishing others' private information, and any conduct that would reasonably
be considered inappropriate in a professional setting. Requests to use this
project for unauthorized or illegal activity are also not welcome.

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported to the
project maintainers via the contact on the repository profile. Maintainers will
review and investigate all complaints and respond as they deem appropriate, and
are obligated to respect the privacy and security of the reporter.
